## Review rules
- Do not assume that publicly accessible assets are free for commercial use.
- Keep license text/attribution where required and retain the source/version.
- Review Flutter package licenses and transitive dependencies before release.
- Treat unknown license terms as `REVIEW` and resolve before distribution.
- This inventory is a process aid, not legal advice.
